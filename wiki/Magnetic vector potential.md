# Magnetic vector potential

**Summary**: A with B equal to curl A.

## Results (lects 19-20)
$$B=\nabla\times A$$
Flux: $\int B\cdot ds=\oint A\cdot d\ell$. Magnetic dipole far field: $A=\mu_0m\times a_R/4\pi R^2$, $m=IS$. For time-varying fields $E=-\nabla V-\partial A/\partial t$ (lect_28).
