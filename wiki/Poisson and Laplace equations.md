# Poisson and Laplace equations

**Summary**: Boundary-value problems for the potential and the method of images.

## Equations (lect_12)
$$\nabla^2V=-\rho_v/\varepsilon$$
$$\nabla^2V=0\quad(\rho_v=0)$$
Solutions are unique given boundary conditions.

## Method of images (lect_14)
A charge near a grounded conductor is replaced by an image charge so that the boundary condition holds on the conductor surface.

## Wiki note
[[PINN]] training solves the same PDE with a network ansatz.
