# Electromagnetic wave

**Summary**: Self-sustaining E and H fields that propagate; plane waves in lossless and lossy media, polarization, power, reflection.

## Wave equation
Source-free Maxwell's equations give (lect_29, wiki note on form):
$$\nabla^2E=\mu\varepsilon\,\partial^2E/\partial t^2$$

## Uniform plane wave
$$E=E_0e^{-\gamma z},\qquad \gamma=\alpha+j\beta$$
Lossless: $\beta=\omega\sqrt{\mu\varepsilon}$, $\eta=\sqrt{\mu/\varepsilon}$, $v_p=\omega/\beta$ (lect_30, wiki note).
