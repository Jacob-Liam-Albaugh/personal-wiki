# Wave polarization

**Summary**: Locus of E over time: linear, circular, elliptical.

## Cases (lect_33)
Phase difference $\delta$ between orthogonal components: $0$ or $\pi$ linear; $\pm\pi/2$ with equal amplitude circular; otherwise elliptical (wiki note).
