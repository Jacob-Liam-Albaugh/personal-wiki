# ME697 4.5 - ODE inverse problems

**Summary**: Calibrating an ODE from noisy trajectories is a [[Bayesian inverse problems]] instance. Two worked examples: a harmonic oscillator where only the ratio $k/m$ is [[Identifiability|identifiable]], sampled with NUTS, and a Duffing oscillator fitted with VI and amortized VI.

**File**: `advanced-scientific-machine-learning/book/inverse/odes/`

---

## Topics
- **01 Basics**: only the heading "# Basics" (empty).
- **02 Identifiability**: harmonic oscillator, a class `ProbabilisticDynamicalSystem`, priors $m\sim U(1,2)$, $k\sim U(0.5,3)$ and a noise scale; each is mapped to a standard-normal $\xi$ (normal CDF for uniforms, exp for $\sigma$). Truth $m=k=1.5$, $\sigma=0.05$, 50 observations on $[0,10]$.
- **03 Multiple trajectories**: the [[Duffing oscillator]] with acceleration observations and known noise.

## Key results
- Harmonic oscillator: NUTS traces look like random walks and the $(m,k)$ posterior is a band along $m=k$. Only $\omega^2=k/m$ is identified; reparameterizing in $\omega^2$ removes the problem (source: odes/02). See [[Identifiability]].
- Duffing: per-dataset VI reconstructs better, while [[Amortized VI]] is much faster at inference. Its solution space is a subset of VI's, since the guide must work for all datasets (source: odes/03).
- AVI trains on the expected ELBO over $p(y)$: 1000 training steps on a GPU; diverging trajectories are filtered out.

## Examples
> [!example] Duffing setup (03)
> $$\dot v=-2\xi\omega_nv-\omega_n^2x-\beta x^3+\gamma\sin\omega t$$
> Unknowns $\theta=(x_0,v_0,\omega_n,\xi,\beta)$. Priors: $x_0,v_0\sim N(0,1)$; $\beta\sim N(0,0.5^2)$; $\omega_n\sim\mathrm{LogNormal}(1.2,0.3^2)$; $\xi\sim\mathrm{LogNormal}(-1.9,0.6^2)$. The guide is a full-covariance Gaussian on a transformed space, including $|\det J_T|$.

> [!failure] Wiki note: sign error in odes/02
> The text writes the oscillator as $m\ddot x=kx$, which has exponential solutions. The stated solution uses $\cos(\sqrt{k/m}\,t)$, which needs $m\ddot x=-kx$.
