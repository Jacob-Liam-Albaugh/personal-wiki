## Key results
Nothing is derived in the source. The standard formulation, labelled as a wiki addition:

**Wiki note**: treat the unknown PDE parameter or field $\kappa$ as extra trainable variables and minimize

$$
\mathcal L(\theta,\kappa) = \mathcal L_{\text{data}} + \lambda\,\mathcal L_{\text{PDE}}(\theta,\kappa).
$$

The Bayesian version replaces the point estimate by a posterior over $(\theta,\kappa)$. In the wiki's framing a PINN is a MAP estimate under a [[Physics-informed prior]], and Bayesian PINNs use [[Bayesian neural networks]] inference. Details are on [[PINN]] and [[Information field theory]]. Whether the inverse problem is well posed is a question of [[Identifiability]].
