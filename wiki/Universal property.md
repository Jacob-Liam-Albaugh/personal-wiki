# Universal property

**Summary**: A characterization of an object by a unique-factorization condition, which determines it up to unique isomorphism; products, coproducts, limits and colimits are the standard cases.

---

## Sources

(source: Seven Sketches, Defs. 3.86, 3.92, 6.1, 6.11, 6.19)

## Definition pattern

Product of $A, B$: an object $A\times B$ with projections such that every pair $T \to A$, $T\to B$ factors uniquely through it. Coproduct and pushout are the duals (Defs. 6.11, 6.19); an initial object has a unique map to every object (Def. 6.1). Limits generalize these to diagrams (Def. 3.92, Thm. 3.95).
