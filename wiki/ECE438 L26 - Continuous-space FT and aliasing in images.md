## Review and notes

- Many English terms for visual effects in images: checkerboard, jaggies, false contouring. Often multiple words describe the same artifact; many are imprecise even when precise definitions exist.
- More pixels do not mean better resolution. Any image resizing (interpolation) will affect viewing.
