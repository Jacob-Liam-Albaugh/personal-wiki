# Faraday's law

**Summary**: Changing flux induces EMF.

## Results (lects 25, 27)
$$\mathcal E=-d\Phi_M/dt$$
Differential form (wiki note): $\nabla\times E=-\partial B/\partial t$. Transformer emf from $\partial B/\partial t$, motional emf from $u\times B$; generators, alternators.
