# Gauss's law

**Summary**: Flux of D through a closed surface equals enclosed charge.

## Forms (lect_5)
$$\oint_sD\cdot ds=Q_{enc},\qquad \nabla\cdot D=\rho_v$$
Differential form follows from the divergence theorem.

## Applications (lect_6)
Symmetric charge (sphere, infinite line, sheet) allows $D$ to be pulled out of the integral. Choose a Gaussian surface where $D$ is constant or perpendicular to it.
