# Biot-Savart law and Lorentz force

**Summary**: B from a current element and force on moving charge and current.

## Results (lects 17, 24)
$$B=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\oint\frac{I\,d\ell'\times(r-r')}{|r-r'|^3}$$
$$F=qu\times B,\qquad dF=I\,d\ell\times B$$
Parallel wires: $F'=\mu_0I_1I_2/2\pi d$. Torque on a coil follows from the force on its sides.
