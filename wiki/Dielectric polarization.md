# Dielectric polarization

**Summary**: Bound charge in materials, the displacement field D, and interface conditions.

## Materials (lects 9-10)
- Conductors: $E=0$ inside, charge on the surface, $E$ normal to the surface.
- Dielectrics: polarization $P$ and $D=\varepsilon_0E+P$ (wiki note); linear media $D=\varepsilon E$.

## Boundary conditions (lect_10)
Wiki note, standard:
$$E_{1t}=E_{2t}$$
$$a_n\cdot(D_1-D_2)=\rho_s$$

## Analogue
Magnetic version: [[Magnetic materials]] with $M$, $H$.
