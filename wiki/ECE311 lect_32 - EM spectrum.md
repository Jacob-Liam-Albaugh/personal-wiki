# ECE311 lect_32 - EM spectrum

**Summary**: Overview of the electromagnetic spectrum from radio to gamma rays and where wave behaviour matters.

**Course**: ECE 311 (Electromagnetics I, Purdue, Spring 2024, Prof. D. S. Elliott) · **Lecture date**: Mar 29 (per the schedule) · **File**: `Electromagnetics/Lectures Slides/lect_32.pdf` (8 slides)

---

## Topics
- Frequency and wavelength bands
- Relation c = f lambda
- Applications by band

## Key results
$$c=f\lambda$$
Bands run from radio and microwaves through infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray to gamma rays; low-frequency wave effects are covered by the same Maxwell equations.

## Examples
> [!example] Wavelength scale
> Circuit theory fails when the line length is comparable to $\lambda$, which motivates [[Transmission line]] models.
