# Ampere's law

**Summary**: Circulation of H equals enclosed free current.

## Forms (lects 18, 21)
$$\oint B\cdot d\ell=\mu_0I_{enc}$$
$$\nabla\times H=J_v$$
Symmetric currents (wire, sheet) give $B$ directly. Needs the displacement-current term for time variation ([[Maxwell's equations]]).
