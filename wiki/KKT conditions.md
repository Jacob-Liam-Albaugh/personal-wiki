# KKT conditions

> [!theorem] KKT conditions for an LP (source: BJS full book.pdf, pp. 238-239, eqs. 5.23-5.25)
> For $\min\mathbf c\mathbf x$ s.t. $\mathbf A\mathbf x\ge\mathbf b$, $\mathbf x\ge\mathbf0$, a feasible $\bar{\mathbf x}$ is optimal iff there are $\mathbf w,\mathbf v\ge\mathbf0$ with
> $$\mathbf A\mathbf x\ge\mathbf b,\quad\mathbf x\ge\mathbf0$$
> $$\mathbf w\mathbf A+\mathbf v=\mathbf c$$
> $$\mathbf w(\mathbf A\mathbf x-\mathbf b)=0,\quad\mathbf v\mathbf x=\mathbf0$$

**Intuition**: at an optimum the objective gradient lies in the cone generated by the gradients of the binding constraints. Equivalently, the multipliers $\mathbf w$ are a feasible dual solution that is complementary to $\bar{\mathbf x}$.

## Key facts
- Necessity follows from [[Farkas lemma]]: no feasible direction $\mathbf d$ with $\mathbf c\mathbf d<0$ and $\mathbf G\mathbf d\ge\mathbf0$ (binding rows $\mathbf G$) exists, so $\mathbf u\mathbf G=\mathbf c$ with $\mathbf u\ge\mathbf0$ (BJS eqs. 5.21-5.22, p. 238).
- Sufficiency: for any feasible $\hat{\mathbf x}$, $\mathbf c\hat{\mathbf x}-\mathbf c\bar{\mathbf x}=\sum_{i\in I}w_i(\mathbf a^i\hat{\mathbf x}-b_i)+\sum_{j\in J}v_j\mathbf e_j\hat{\mathbf x}\ge0$ (BJS p. 238).
- The three groups are primal feasibility, dual feasibility and complementary slackness, so for LPs they restate [[LP duality]] (BJS p. 239).
- BJS Example 5.7 shows a point failing the test because the multipliers would have to be negative (BJS p. 239).
- The barrier conditions of the [[Interior point method]] are the KKT conditions of the barrier problems with complementarity relaxed to $\mathbf X\mathbf S\mathbf e=\mu\mathbf e$ (BT Lemma 9.5, p. 421), and the Newton step of primal-dual path following is applied to that perturbed system (BJS ch8, §8.6).
- The out-of-kilter optimality test (Theorem 11.1) is complementary slackness for the bounded-variable network LP ([[BJS ch11 - The out-of-kilter algorithm]]).
- Wiki note: this wiki page covers only the LP case as treated in these two books; nonlinear KKT theory is outside the staged sources.
