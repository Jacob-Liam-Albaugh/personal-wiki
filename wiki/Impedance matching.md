# Impedance matching

**Summary**: Eliminating reflection by quarter-wave sections and stubs.

## Methods (lect_43)
$$R_{02}^2=Z_LR_0$$
Single-stub matching: $Y_{in}=Y_1+Y_{st}$. Choose position for $\mathrm{Re}\,Y_1=Y_0$, then cancel the susceptance.
