# Magnetostatics

**Summary**: Fields of steady currents: Biot-Savart and Ampere laws, vector potential, magnetic materials, inductance and forces.

## Governing equations
$$\nabla\cdot B=0,\qquad \nabla\times H=J_v$$
$$B=\nabla\times A,\qquad B=\mu H$$

## Route through the course
- Source and force: [[Biot-Savart law and Lorentz force]].
- Symmetric currents: [[Ampere's law]].
- Potential and dipole: [[Magnetic vector potential]].
- Materials, H and interfaces: [[Magnetic materials]].
- Energy, flux linkage: [[Inductance]].
- Where statics fails: [[Faraday's law]], [[Maxwell's equations]].

## Wiki note
Electric counterpart: [[Electrostatics]]; there are no isolated magnetic charges ($\nabla\cdot B=0$).
