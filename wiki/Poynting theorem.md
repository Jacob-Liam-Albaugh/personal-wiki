# Poynting theorem

**Summary**: Conservation of electromagnetic energy and the power flow vector.

## Results (lects 34-35)
$$S=E\times H$$
Time-harmonic average $S_{av}=\tfrac12\mathrm{Re}(E\times H^*)$. Energy densities $\tfrac12E\cdot D$, $\tfrac12H\cdot B$; loss $E\cdot J$. Wiki note: forms stated, not derived on this page.
