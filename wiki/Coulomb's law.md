# Coulomb's law

**Summary**: Force and field of point charges and continuous charge distributions.

## Statement (lect_4)
Force between point charges $q_1,q_2$ separated by $R$ (wiki note, standard form):
$$F=\frac{q_1q_2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0R^2}a_R$$
Field of a charge: $E=F/q$. Superposition applies to line, surface and volume charge.

## Use
Direct integration is practical only for simple geometry; symmetry problems use [[Gauss's law]], others use [[Electric potential]].
