# ECE311 lect_22 - Magnetic boundary conditions and inductance

**Summary**: Boundary conditions for B and H across an interface, refraction of field lines, and an introduction to inductance.

**Course**: ECE 311 (Electromagnetics I, Purdue, Spring 2024, Prof. D. S. Elliott) · **Lecture date**: Feb 28 (per the schedule) · **File**: `Electromagnetics/Lectures Slides/lect_22.pdf` (9 slides)

---

## Topics
- Normal component of B
- Tangential component of H and surface current
- Inductance

## Key results
Boundary conditions (p. 1):
$$B_{1n}=B_{2n},\qquad a_{n2}\times(H_1-H_2)=J_s$$
With no surface current and linear media, $\mu_1H_{1n}=\mu_2H_{2n}$ and $H_{1t}=H_{2t}$, so the angles of field lines on the two sides are related by the permeabilities.
True surface currents appear only on perfect conductors; for dielectrics and imperfect conductors $H_t$ is continuous.
