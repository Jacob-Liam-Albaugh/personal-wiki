# Ohm's law and current density

**Summary**: Conduction current J, conductivity and Joule heating.

## Results (lects 15-16)
$$J_v=\rho_vu,\qquad J_v=\sigma E$$
$$\wp=\int E\cdot J_v\,dv$$
Uniform case: $R=V/I$, $\wp=VI$. Charge conservation: $\nabla\cdot J_v=-\partial\rho_v/\partial t$. Kirchhoff's laws appear in field form (lect_15).
