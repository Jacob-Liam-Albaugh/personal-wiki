# Electric potential

**Summary**: Scalar potential V, the dipole, and the curl-free property of E.

## Definition (lect_7)
$$V=-\int E\cdot d\ell,\qquad E=-\nabla V$$
Possible because $\nabla\times E=0$ (Stokes' theorem).

## Dipole (lect_8)
Two opposite charges give a potential falling as $1/R^2$ and a field falling as $1/R^3$ (wiki note on exponents).
