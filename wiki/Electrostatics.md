# Electrostatics

**Summary**: Fields of stationary charge: Coulomb and Gauss laws, potential, materials, energy, boundary-value problems and capacitance.

## Governing equations
$$\nabla\cdot D=\rho_v,\qquad \nabla\times E=0$$
$$E=-\nabla V,\qquad D=\varepsilon E$$
Combined: $\nabla^2V=-\rho_v/\varepsilon$ ([[Poisson and Laplace equations]]).

## Route through the course
- Charges and fields: [[Coulomb's law]], [[Gauss's law]].
- Potential and dipole: [[Electric potential]].
- Materials and interfaces: [[Dielectric polarization]] (D, boundary conditions), conductors.
- Energy and forces: [[Electrostatic energy]].
- Boundary-value problems: [[Poisson and Laplace equations]], method of images.
- Circuit quantity: [[Capacitance]].
- Currents: [[Ohm's law and current density]].

## Wiki note
Time-varying extension: [[Maxwell's equations]]. Magnetic counterpart: [[Magnetostatics]].
