# Farkas lemma

> [!theorem] Farkas' lemma (source: MIT (ch 1-5).pdf, p. 165, Thm 4.6)
> Exactly one holds: (a) there is $\mathbf x\ge\mathbf0$ with $\mathbf A\mathbf x=\mathbf b$; (b) there is $\mathbf p$ with $\mathbf p'\mathbf A\ge\mathbf0'$ and $\mathbf p'\mathbf b<0$.

> [!theorem] Farkas' lemma, BJS form (source: BJS full book.pdf, p. 234, Lemma 5.1)
> Exactly one of the systems has a solution:
> $$\text{System 1: }\ \mathbf A\mathbf x\ge\mathbf0,\ \ \mathbf c\mathbf x<0$$
> $$\text{System 2: }\ \mathbf w\mathbf A=\mathbf c,\ \ \mathbf w\ge\mathbf0$$

**Intuition**: either $\mathbf b$ lies in the cone generated by the columns of $\mathbf A$, or a hyperplane separates it from that cone. A vector in (b) certifies infeasibility. In BJS form System 2 says $\mathbf c$ is in the cone generated by the rows of $\mathbf A$ (BJS ch5, p. 236-237).

## Key facts
- Proof from [[LP duality]]: if (b) held with a feasible $\mathbf x$, then $\mathbf p'\mathbf b=\mathbf p'\mathbf A\mathbf x\ge0$. If (a) fails, the dual of "maximize $\mathbf 0'\mathbf x$" is feasible ($\mathbf p=\mathbf 0$) and the primal is infeasible, so the dual is unbounded, which yields the vector in (b) (BT p. 165).
- Other proofs: separating hyperplane theorem for a closed convex set and a point outside it (BT Thm 4.11, p. 170); constructively from the [[Simplex method]] (BJS ch5, §5.3).
- Variants for inequality systems $\mathbf A\mathbf x\le\mathbf b$ (BT Cor. 4.3, Thm 4.7, pp. 166-167). BT read it as absence of arbitrage in a one-period market (Thm 4.8).
- Used to derive the [[KKT conditions]]: no improving feasible direction at an optimum means the objective gradient lies in the cone of binding-constraint gradients (BJS ch5, §5.4).
