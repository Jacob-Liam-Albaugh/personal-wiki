# ECE311 lect_21 - Magnetic field H and materials

**Summary**: Equivalent currents modify Ampere's law and define H; diamagnetic, paramagnetic and ferromagnetic materials.

**Course**: ECE 311 (Electromagnetics I, Purdue, Spring 2024, Prof. D. S. Elliott) · **Lecture date**: Feb 26 (per the schedule) · **File**: `Electromagnetics/Lectures Slides/lect_21.pdf` (8 slides)

---

## Topics
- Equivalent current density $\nabla\times M$
- Definition of H
- Material types

## Key results
Modified Ampere law (p. 1), valid in all media for static fields:
$$H=\frac{B}{\mu_0}-M\ \ (\mathrm{A/m}),\qquad \nabla\times H=J_v$$
Linear media: $B=\mu H$ (stated in lect_25). A displacement-current term must still be added for time-varying fields.

## Examples
> [!example] Physical meaning (p. 1)
> $B$ is the total field, $M$ the magnetic dipole moment density, and $H$ is sourced by free currents only - the magnetic mirror of $E$, $P$, $D$.
