## Nagel-Schreckenberg traffic

- **Source**: planned, not in the books or lab papers
- **Gap filled**: Cellular automaton
- **Class**: cellular automaton
- **Forward model**: Cars on a ring of cells: accelerate by one, brake to the gap ahead, slow at random with probability p, then move.
- **Data**: NGSIM vehicle trajectories, 11.85 million rows from US-101, I-80 and other sites: position, speed, lane, leader and follower. CC BY-SA 3.0.
- **Data origin**: real
