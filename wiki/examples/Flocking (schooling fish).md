## Flocking (schooling fish)

- **Source**: planned, not in the books or lab papers
- **Gap filled**: Interacting particles
- **Class**: ODE network
- **Forward model**: Vicsek: each agent turns toward the mean heading of its neighbours, plus noise. Cucker-Smale: velocities relax toward neighbours with distance-weighted coupling.
- **Data**: Tracked 2D trajectories: 6 recordings of 8 fish (30 min) and 2 of 39 fish (60 min). CC BY 4.0.
- **Data origin**: real
- **Links** (each opened by script on 2026-11-19; "resolved" means the page loaded, not that the contents were read):
