## Lévy-process asset returns

- **Source**: planned, not in the books or lab papers
- **Gap filled**: Jump process
- **Class**: jump process
- **Forward model**: Log-price is a pure-jump Lévy process: variance gamma, normal inverse Gaussian or CGMY.
- **Data**: Daily US market excess return from July 1926, about 25,000 days.
- **Data origin**: real
- **Links** (each opened by script on 2026-11-19; "resolved" means the page loaded, not that the contents were read):
