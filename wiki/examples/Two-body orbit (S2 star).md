## Two-body orbit (S2 star)

- **Source**: planned, not in the books or lab papers
- **Gap filled**: Conservative
- **Class**: ODE
- **Forward model**: Kepler orbit: r'' = -G M r / |r|^3, with six orbital elements plus the central mass and distance.
- **Data**: About 25 years of astrometry and radial velocities of the star S2 around Sgr A*; the tables are inside the paper.
- **Data origin**: real
- **Links** (each opened by script on 2026-11-19; "resolved" means the page loaded, not that the contents were read):
