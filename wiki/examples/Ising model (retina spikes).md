## Ising model (retina spikes)

- **Source**: planned, not in the books or lab papers
- **Gap filled**: Markov chain
- **Class**: Ising model
- **Forward model**: P(s) proportional to exp(sum_i h_i s_i + sum_ij J_ij s_i s_j) over binary spike patterns.
- **Data**: 160 salamander retinal ganglion cells, 297 repeats of a 19 s movie, binary spikes in 20 ms bins. CC0.
- **Data origin**: real
- **Links** (each opened by script on 2026-11-19; "resolved" means the page loaded, not that the contents were read):
  - [ISTA record 5562](https://research-explorer.ista.ac.at/record/5562): resolved
- **Notes**: Real binary spin patterns. Paper: Tkačik et al. 2014. No real magnetisation data was found.
