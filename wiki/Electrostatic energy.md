# Electrostatic energy

**Summary**: Energy stored in charge distributions and electric fields, and forces from it.

## Results (lect_11)
Wiki note, standard forms:
$$W_e=\tfrac12\int E\cdot D\,dv,\qquad w_e=\tfrac12E\cdot D$$
Forces follow from the change of stored energy with displacement. Magnetic counterpart: $w_m=\tfrac12H\cdot B$ ([[Inductance]]). Sphere energy factor not verified against slides.
