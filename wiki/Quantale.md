# Quantale

**Summary**: A closed symmetric monoidal preorder that has all joins; the right base for enrichment because $\mathcal V$-matrices then compose.

---

> [!definition] Quantale
> A symmetric monoidal preorder $(\mathcal V,\le,I,\otimes)$ that is closed (Def. 2.79, a right adjoint $\multimap$ to $\otimes$) and has all joins (Def. 2.90). (source: Seven Sketches, Defs. 2.79, 2.90)

## Intuition

Examples: Bool, Cost. In a quantale, $\mathcal V$-matrices multiply (Def. 2.100) and $\mathcal V$-categories can be presented by generators. Closure is an instance of [[Adjunction]].

## Where it appears

[[Seven Sketches ch2 - Resource theories]]; basis for [[Enriched category]] and [[Profunctor]].
