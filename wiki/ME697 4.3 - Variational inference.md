# ME697 4.3 - Variational inference

**Summary**: [[Variational inference]] replaces sampling by optimization: pick a parametric family $q_\phi$, and minimize the [[KL divergence]] to the posterior, which is the same as maximizing the [[ELBO]]. The chapter covers guide families, the derivation, the [[Reparameterization trick]], and a full-rank Gaussian guide on the [[Catalysis problem]].

**File**: `advanced-scientific-machine-learning/book/inverse/vi/`

---

## Topics
- **01 Basics**:
  - Guides: diagonal Gaussian (with $\lambda=\log\sigma$), low-rank, full-rank via a Cholesky factor with exponentiated diagonal, transformed guides (log for positive, logit for $[0,1]$, with the Jacobian), Gamma and Beta, composite and structured guides.
  - KL properties, with a Jensen proof of non-negativity.
  - The ELBO derivation: $\mathrm{KL}=\log p(y)-\mathrm{ELBO}$.
  - Reparameterization $x=g_\phi(\epsilon)=\mu+L\epsilon$ and the sample-average loss estimator.
- **02 Catalysis**: every unknown is mapped to a standard-normal $x\in\mathbb R^6$ through a transform $T$ (rates lognormal with $\gamma=2$; the noise variance exponential, via normal CDF and inverse CDF). The guide is an Equinox `FullRankGaussianGuide` with 27 parameters (6 means, 21 Cholesky entries). Adam, learning rate $5\cdot10^{-2}$, batch 5, 3000 steps.
- **03 3D reconstruction**: a stub (title only).

## Key results
- The ELBO needs only the joint $p(y,x)$, not the evidence.
- The reparameterized gradient lets autodiff differentiate through the sampling.
- Different parts of the state space can fail in the ODE solver; the notebook uses diffrax `Event(state_has_nan)` so bad regions are skipped (source: vi/02).
- Exercises in vi/02 ask how the posterior changes with the noise rate $\lambda$ and the prior scale $\gamma$.
