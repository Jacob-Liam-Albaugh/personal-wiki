# Capacitance

**Summary**: Charge-to-voltage ratio of a conductor pair and its calculation.

## Definition (lect_14)
$$C=Q/V$$
Find $E$ from [[Gauss's law]] or by solving Laplace's equation, integrate to $V$, divide. Stored energy $\tfrac12CV^2$ ([[Electrostatic energy]]). Line capacitance per unit length gives $C'$ of a [[Transmission line]].
