# ECE311 lect_18 - Ampere's law

**Summary**: Divergence and curl of B for static fields, the integral Ampere's law and its use to find B for symmetric currents.

**Course**: ECE 311 (Electromagnetics I, Purdue, Spring 2024, Prof. D. S. Elliott) · **Lecture date**: Feb 19 (per the schedule) · **File**: `Electromagnetics/Lectures Slides/lect_18.pdf` (7 slides)

---

## Topics
- No magnetic charges: $\nabla\cdot B=0$
- Ampere's law in differential and integral form
- Applications: straight wire, current sheet

## Key results
Static, vacuum (pp. 1-2):
$$\nabla\cdot B=0,\qquad \nabla\times B=\mu_0J_v$$
$$\oint_cB\cdot d\ell=\mu_0I_{enc}$$
Straight wire of radius $a$, uniform current $I$ (p. 2): $B_\varphi=\mu_0I/2\pi\rho$ outside and $\mu_0I\rho/2\pi a^2$ inside.

## Examples
> [!example] Infinite current sheet (p. 3)
> Example 18-2 uses a rectangular Amperian loop around a sheet of uniform surface current; the field is uniform on each side.
