## Review and notes

- $f(x,y)$ is a continuous-space image; $f(m,n)$ is a discrete-space or digital image (same notation for both).
- $(M,N)$ is the size; $L=2^k$ is the number of quantization levels.
- A color image $\mathbf f(m,n)$ has each pixel a vector. Many cameras default to RGB; OpenCV defaults to BGR.
(source: L25 Mar 26, 2025.pdf, p. 1)
