# Magnetic materials

**Summary**: Magnetization, H and magnetic boundary conditions.

## Results (lects 20-22)
$$H=B/\mu_0-M,\qquad J_{eff}=\nabla\times M$$
Boundary: $B_{1n}=B_{2n}$ and $a_n\times(H_1-H_2)=J_s$. Types: diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic. Electric analogue: [[Dielectric polarization]].
