# Reflection and transmission of waves

**Summary**: Behaviour of a plane wave at an interface and standing waves.

## Normal incidence (lects 36-37)
$$\Gamma=\frac{\eta_2-\eta_1}{\eta_2+\eta_1},\qquad \tau=1+\Gamma$$
Standing waves: nulls and maxima $\lambda/2$ apart, SWR $=(1+|\Gamma|)/(1-|\Gamma|)$ (wiki note). Same algebra on a [[Transmission line]].
